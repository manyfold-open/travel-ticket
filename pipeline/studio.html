How it works

Trip Brief turns your sentence into a structured request; Timezone and Local Discovery work in parallel; Composer builds the itinerary; the renderer prints the cover and daily tickets.

A live run usually takes 5–8 minutes, with web research taking most of the time. Each agent status is recorded honestly in the handbook.

Gmail and Calendar connectors are optional and appear as skipped when unavailable.
